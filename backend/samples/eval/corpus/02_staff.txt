CS 401 Machine Learning — Teaching Staff, Contact and Office Hours
===================================================================

Who teaches CS 401, when they are available, and how to reach them. This
document is the authoritative source for contact information; details change
term to term and the version in the course site is not kept in sync.


1. Instructor
-------------

Dr. Amara Okonkwo is the instructor of record for CS 401. Dr. Okonkwo writes
the lectures, sets the examinations, and has final authority over grading
disputes that cannot be resolved with the teaching assistants.

  Email: a.okonkwo@example.edu
  Office: Gates Building 512
  Pronouns: she/her

Dr. Okonkwo holds office hours in Gates 512 and is also reachable by
appointment. She asks that you email rather than arrive unannounced, because
she is frequently in meetings during the advertised hours.

3. Office hours
---------------

Instructor office hours are held on Wednesdays from 2pm to 4pm in Gates 512.
The room booking runs the full two hours; if it is occupied the door sign will
show an alternative room number in the same building.

TA office hours are held on Tuesdays from 4pm to 6pm in Gates 411, and on
Fridays from 10am to noon in Gates 411. Daniel Okoye also holds a Monday
session from 1pm to 3pm in Gates 410 for his sections only.

All office hours are drop-in. You do not need to book a slot, and you do not
need to prepare anything in advance. If a session is unusually crowded, ask
for a one-to-one follow-up by email rather than waiting.


4. How to get an answer quickly
-------------------------------

Most questions are answered faster by email than by waiting for office hours,
because email can be answered outside the advertised window. Include the
following so the answer can be given in one reply rather than two:

  - the assignment number and the specific question or part number
  - what you tried, and what you expected to happen instead
  - whether the question is about the concept or only about the marking

Questions about a grading decision that you believe is wrong should be emailed
rather than raised in office hours, so that the staff member can look at the
specific work before replying. Grades are released on the course site, and
individual marks are not discussed in public or in the group chat.


5. Other staff
--------------

Priya Raman also coordinates the weekly optional practice sessions held on
Wednesday mornings before the lecture. The sessions are not recorded and are
not graded.

Questions that concern accommodations, extensions for medical reasons, or
registration should go to the department advising office rather than to the
teaching staff, who cannot process them. The advising office handles
registration changes, withdrawal deadlines, and accommodation letters.

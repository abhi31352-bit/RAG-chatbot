CS 401 Machine Learning — Weekly Schedule
==========================================

What is covered each week, and what is due. The schedule is fixed for the term
and is not adjusted for individual sections; all sections cover the same
material in the same order.


Weeks 1 to 7
------------

  Week 1 ... Supervised learning, linear and logistic regression.
            Homework 1 released.
  Week 2 ... Regularisation, overfitting, L1 versus L2, cross-validation.
            Homework 2 released. Project teams formed.
  Week 3 ... Gradient descent, learning rates, momentum, SGD variants.
            Homework 3 released.
  Week 4 ... Evaluation metrics, precision, recall, F1, imbalanced data.
            Homework 4 released.
  Week 5 ... Decision trees, random forests, bagging and boosting.
            Project milestone 1 due at the end of this week.
            Homework 5 released.
  Week 6 ... Clustering, k-means, DBSCAN, choosing the number of clusters.
            Homework 6 released.
  Week 7 ... Midterm examination. No lecture; the midterm is in the usual slot.

Homework 7 is released in week 8 and covers weeks 6 and 7. There is no
homework in the week of the midterm.


Weeks 8 to 14
-------------

  Week 8 ...... Data pipelines and feature engineering. Homework 8 released.
  Week 9 ...... Neural networks and backpropagation. Homework 9 released.
  Week 10 ..... Convolutional models. Homework 10 released.
  Week 11 ..... Project milestone 2 due at the end of this week. No new
                homework; the week is dedicated to the milestone.
  Week 12 ..... Recurrent models and attention. Guest lecture.
  Week 13 ..... Project presentations in the allocated lecture slots.
                Homework 11 released.
  Week 14 ..... Final examination. Catch-up project presentations for teams
                that missed their allocated slot. Project milestone 4 due.


Examinations
------------

The midterm examination takes place in week 7 and covers weeks 1 to 6
inclusive. It is a single 90-minute sitting. There is a 10-minute reading time
before writing begins, which does not count towards the 90 minutes.

The final examination takes place in week 14 and is cumulative, covering the
whole course including the material from weeks 8 to 12. It is a single
2-hour sitting, again preceded by a reading time.


Notes on the schedule
---------------------

The guest lecture in week 12 is on attention mechanisms and is assessed. The
lecturer for that week is not the course instructor, but the material is
examinable and the notes are provided like any other week.

There is no lecture in week 14. The final examination occupies the usual slot
and the catch-up presentations run in the afternoon.

The schedule leaves weeks 11 and 13 free of new homework so that milestone and
presentation work is possible. This is a firm arrangement and the homework
deadlines do not move to accommodate the project.

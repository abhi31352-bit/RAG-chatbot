CS 401 Machine Learning — Grade Breakdown
=========================================

This document sets out exactly how your final grade in CS 401 is computed.
Every component, its weight, and the rules that apply to it are listed below.
If anything here is unclear, raise it with the teaching staff before the first
assessment rather than after the grade is released.


1. Component weights
--------------------

The course grade is a weighted sum of five components. The weights are fixed
for the whole term and are not adjusted for individual students.

  Final written examination ............ 40 percent
  Term project ........................ 30 percent
  Homework assignments (10 of them) ... 20 percent
  Midterm examination .................. 10 percent
  Participation and attendance ........  0 percent

Homework is graded per assignment and then averaged across the ten
assignments, so a single weak week does not dominate the homework component.
The midterm is a single sitting in week 7 and covers material from weeks 1
through 6. The final examination is a single sitting in the last week of
term and is cumulative, covering all material from the whole course.


2. The grade calculation
------------------------

Your final letter grade is computed from the weighted percentage using the
scale below. There is no rounding at any intermediate step; the weighted
percentage is carried to two decimal places and then mapped to a letter.

  93.00 and above ........ A
  90.00 to 92.99 ......... A-
  87.00 to 89.99 ......... B+
  83.00 to 86.99 ......... B
  80.00 to 82.99 ......... B-
  77.00 to 79.99 ......... C+
  73.00 to 76.99 ......... C
  70.00 to 72.99 ......... C-
  below 70.00 ............ F

The course does not apply a curve. The scale above is the scale used.


3. Late work
------------

Homework submitted after the published deadline loses 10 percent of the
possible mark for each day it is late. The penalty is applied per calendar
day, including weekends and holidays, and it caps at 50 percent of the
possible mark. In other words, work handed in five or more days late receives
half credit at best.

There is no late penalty on the term project, but the project has hard
milestone dates instead. Missing a project milestone does not attract a
percentage penalty; it removes the milestone from the project score entirely.
The project is scored out of 100 points across four milestones, so losing one
milestone costs 25 points of the project component.

Extensions are granted only for documented medical reasons and only when
requested before the deadline. Extensions are arranged through the
department's advising office, not through the teaching staff.


4. Exemptions and regrades
--------------------------

Students who sat a previous offering of CS 401 may apply for an exemption from
the final examination on the basis of a comparable prior result. Applications
are considered once, before week 1, and an exemption replaces the examination
component with a score awarded from the prior transcript. Because the
examination carries 40 percent of the grade, an exemption has a large effect
and is not granted lightly.

Regrades for the midterm examination and for individual homework assignments
may be requested within seven days of the relevant result being posted. A
regrade request must name a specific question or part, not express general
disagreement with the marking scheme. Regrades of the final examination are
handled by the university process and not by the course.


5. What is not counted
---------------------

Attendance at lectures is not graded. The participation component carries zero
weight and exists only so that teaching staff can record engagement. You can
miss lectures without affecting your grade, though you remain responsible for
material covered in them.

Peer review of other students' code is part of the project component and is
graded. It is worth 10 of the 100 project points. Not submitting peer reviews
costs those 10 points.

The optional practice problems published each week are not collected and are
not graded. They exist purely as practice.

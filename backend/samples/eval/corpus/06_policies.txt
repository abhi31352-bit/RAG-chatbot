CS 401 Machine Learning — Course Policies
=========================================

Attendance, conduct, use of external tools, and accommodations. These policies
apply to every student in the course regardless of section.


1. Attendance
-------------

Lectures are not recorded and attendance is not taken for grading purposes.
The participation component of the grade carries zero weight, so missing a
lecture has no effect on your mark.

You remain responsible for everything covered in a lecture you miss. Slides
are posted after each lecture, but the slides are an outline and the worked
examples only exist in the lecture itself.

Tutorials are also optional. The weekly practice sessions are optional and
ungraded.


2. Academic integrity
--------------------

The work you submit must be your own. The following are permitted:

  - discussing the ideas behind an assignment with other students
  - reading documentation, tutorials, and reference implementations
  - using a search engine to look up a concept you do not understand
  - asking a teaching assistant or the instructor for help

The following are not permitted:

  - submitting code or written answers produced by another student
  - sharing your submission with another student, in any form
  - giving code or answers to another student, in any form
  - presenting work you did not do as your own in the project report

Discussing an assignment with another student is permitted. Submitting their
answer is not. The distinction is about what you hand in, not about what you
talked about beforehand.


3. Use of generative AI tools
-----------------------------

Use of large language model assistants is permitted in this course, under
conditions. The rules are specific:

You may use an AI assistant to explain a concept, to generate ideas, to
debug your own code, and to improve the clarity of prose you have already
written. You may not use an assistant to produce a solution to an assessed
problem and submit it, and you may not use one to write your project report.

If you use an assistant at all on an assessed piece of work, you must say so in
a one-line comment at the top of the file, naming the tool and the stage at
which you used it. This is a disclosure requirement, not a permission
requirement. Failing to disclose use is treated as misconduct; disclosing it
is never itself penalised.

The reason for the disclosure rule is that the course is partly assessing
whether you can do the work unaided. A solution you could not have produced
unaided does not demonstrate that you have learned anything.


4. Accommodations
-----------------

Students with a disability that affects assessment should arrange
accommodations through the department advising office, well before the first
assessment. Accommodations include additional time, a separate room, and
alternative assessment formats.

Accommodations are arranged with the advising office and not with the teaching
staff, who cannot process them. Letters must be in place before the midterm in
week 7, and the advising office needs several weeks of notice to arrange a
separate room.

Tell the teaching staff about an accommodation as soon as it is confirmed so
that marking arrangements can be planned, but the paperwork itself goes to the
advising office.


5. Withdrawal and deadlines
---------------------------

The standard university withdrawal deadline applies. Withdrawal after that
deadline requires documented grounds and is at the discretion of the department.

Adding and dropping the course during the add-drop period has no academic
consequences. After the add-drop period the course appears on your transcript
with a grade, including an F.

There is no penalty for withdrawing before the project is due.

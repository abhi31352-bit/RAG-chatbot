## Assessment dates

- **Midterm 1** is held in week 3, on the second Tuesday of the term.
- **Midterm 2** is held in week 5, on the fifth Tuesday of the term.
- The **project proposal** is due at the end of week 4.
- The **final project report** is due at 11:59pm on the last day of the term.
- The **final examination** is in the normal exam period, two hours long, and
  covers all ten weeks with roughly 40 percent of the marks on the material from
  the second half of the course.

## Holidays

There is no class on the week 6 Monday for the spring holiday weekend. The week
6 lecture material is not rescheduled; read it in advance.

Office hours run every Tuesday from 2pm to 4pm in Gates 412 throughout the term,
including the week 10 review period. The additional Thursday hour with the
teaching assistant runs only in weeks 1 through 8.

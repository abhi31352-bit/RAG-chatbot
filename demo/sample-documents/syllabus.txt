COURSE GRADING

The final examination accounts for 40 percent of the course grade. Weekly
problem sets account for 30 percent, the term project accounts for 20 percent,
and class participation accounts for 10 percent.

Late problem set submissions lose 10 percent of the marks per day, up to a
maximum penalty of 50 percent. Work submitted more than seven days late is not
accepted at all.

The lowest of your two midterm scores is dropped when computing the midterm
component of the grade. There is no make-up exam; if you miss a midterm
entirely you receive a zero for that exam.

PROJECT

The term project is a group assignment of two or three students. Groups must
be formed by the end of week three. The project proposal is due at the end of
week four and is worth five percent of the final course grade as a standalone
mark. A proposal that is late receives no credit for that component.

The final project report is due at 11:59pm on the last day of the term. Code
submitted after the deadline will not be evaluated.

Lecture attendance is not taken and is not graded directly, but the material
examined assumes you have seen each week's lecture. Two unexcused absences from
a scheduled midterm is grounds for failing the course under university policy.
